The Allies · Bond Street, London

You found it.

Most people walk past the bench without looking up. You stopped, and then you went looking for whatever the code was hiding. That puts you in a very small group, and this page exists for that group.

In May 2026 the bronze of Churchill and Roosevelt came off its plinth on New Bond Street and was taken away into storage while the street was rebuilt around it. I was on the pavement with a camera, as a passer-by, not a picture desk. Fourteen frames of a morning that happened once.

This is the first edition of the archive. There will be others.

A word on the numbers

Fifty prints are signed and numbered. That is the edition, and it does not get reprinted. The first ten carry a lower number on the certificate, which is why they are priced apart from the rest.

Each print is made to order and posted rolled, with postage included in the UK. A certificate of authenticity is numbered to match the print. Every frame in the archive has the faces of the site workers obscured - the bronze faces are never touched.

What happens next

One edition a month, found the same way you found this one. If you would rather be told, follow the archive and it will reach you first.
